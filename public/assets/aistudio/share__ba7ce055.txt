
ORION — OFFICIAL BRAND ESTABLISHMENT, NAME TRANSFORMATION AND APPLICATION-WIDE REBRANDING

PROJECT DIRECTIVE

We are officially transforming the existing Kọ́kọ́Learn application into ORION.

ORION is the permanent product identity of this application and its entire educational ecosystem.

This is a comprehensive brand transformation and application-wide rebranding exercise. It is NOT a request to build a new application, redesign the backend, or replace the existing technical foundation.

The objective is to establish ORION as a distinctive, recognisable, premium AI-powered educational technology brand and integrate that identity consistently throughout the entire existing application.

The attached ORION Education Brand Identity Board is the authoritative visual reference.

IMPORTANT: Follow the specific logo and wordmark selections below. Do not independently reinterpret, replace or redesign them.

---

1. OFFICIAL BRAND IDENTITY

Official brand name: ORION

Official tagline: Discover beyond what you know.

Brand positioning: An AI-powered learning ecosystem for Nigeria and beyond.

Brand promise: Make quality learning more accessible, intelligent, engaging and effective.

Brand personality:

- Intelligent.
- Ambitious.
- Welcoming.
- Innovative.
- Trustworthy.
- Inclusive.
- Forward-looking.

ORION should feel like a complete learning environment rather than an ordinary school management application with an AI chatbot attached.

It must appeal to students, teachers, schools, parents and independent learners while remaining capable of expanding into tertiary education and lifelong learning.

2. CRITICAL — OFFICIAL ORION WORDMARK

This is one of the most important requirements of the entire branding exercise.

Use the exact visual lettering of the word ORION shown prominently in the attached ORION Education Brand Identity Board.

Specifically, reference the large uppercase ORION lettering positioned immediately above:

"Discover beyond what you know."

That exact lettering is the official ORION wordmark.

Do not interpret this as a general instruction to use Montserrat Bold, Montserrat ExtraBold or another similar font.

We want the actual visual character of the lettering reproduced as closely as possible.

Required characteristics

Preserve:

- The exact uppercase appearance.
- The bold geometric construction.
- The distinctive character shapes.
- The proportions and widths of the letters.
- The thickness and weight.
- The spacing between characters.
- The overall visual balance and alignment.
- The recognisable appearance of the complete word.

The wordmark must look like the lettering in the attached reference, not simply the word ORION typed in a standard font.

Technical implementation

Treat the wordmark as a custom brand asset.

If the original vector artwork or exact font is unavailable, recreate the lettering faithfully using vector outlines or an equivalent scalable format.

Produce clean, high-resolution assets suitable for different screen sizes and resolutions.

Maintain:

- Ivory wordmark for dark forest-green surfaces.
- Forest-green wordmark for appropriate light surfaces.
- Consistent proportions across all placements.

Do not stretch, distort, compress, italicise or arbitrarily modify the wordmark.

Application-wide wordmark consistency

Use the approved wordmark consistently on:

- Landing page.
- Main application header.
- Desktop navigation.
- Mobile navigation.
- Login and registration screens.
- Loading and splash screens.
- Student workspace.
- Teacher workspace.
- School administrator workspace.
- Independent learner workspace.
- PWA identity and application presentation.
- Other prominent brand placements.

The application name must always be presented as ORION in the approved uppercase wordmark style wherever a prominent brand presentation is appropriate.

Ordinary text references to the product may use the text ORION, but the primary visual brand presentation must use the custom wordmark.
